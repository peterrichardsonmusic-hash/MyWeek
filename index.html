Activities

Each bar shows how many of the week’s boxes an activity fills.

Set a weekly target for an activity under Edit activities, and a marker on its bar shows the target.

The Four Pillars represent the four key areas of life that deserve your time and attention: Work, Passion & Pursuit, Life & Love, and Health. They provide a simple way to think about how you spend your time and where it is being invested.

The pillars aren’t rigid categories, and many activities will naturally support more than one. What matters is that each has a place in your life.

Each box counts toward every pillar its activity strengthens. Hover over or tap a pillar name below for its meaning.
